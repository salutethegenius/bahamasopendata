The historic moment

52 years of deficits.
One balanced budget.

Every year since Bahamian independence in 1973, the government spent more than it earned. Each bar below represents a year in the red. Until now.

Deficit year
Surplus — FY2025/26
1973 — Independence 1990 2000 2010 2020 2025/26 ★
2025/26 — The Bahamas records its first budget surplus since independence. A $75.5M surplus on a $3.8B budget. National debt-to-GDP falls from 88.7% to 68.9%. For the first time in 52 years, the government earned more than it spent. This is what fiscal discipline looks like — and BahamasOpenData will be the public record of it.
